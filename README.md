# 🚀 OrbitGuard AI — Space Debris Collision Detection & Avoidance

> **AI-powered decision-support system for safer and more sustainable space operations.**

OrbitGuard AI is designed to help monitor orbital objects, assess collision risk, and support timely maneuver decisions using orbital mechanics, AI-based risk assessment, uncertainty analysis, and maneuver optimization.

## 🎯 Problem

The growing number of satellites and orbital debris is increasing congestion around Earth. Existing collision-avoidance workflows can be reactive, time-consuming, and dependent on manual analysis.

A collision can damage or destroy spacecraft and, in severe cases, contribute to cascading debris generation known as the **Kessler Syndrome**.

## 💡 Our Solution

OrbitGuard AI acts as an intelligent **space-traffic decision-support system**.

It combines:

- TLE and orbital data
- SGP4-based orbit propagation
- Conjunction screening
- Miss-distance and relative-motion analysis
- AI-based collision-risk assessment
- Monte Carlo uncertainty analysis
- Maneuver optimization
- Decision-support alerts for operators

> **The final maneuver decision remains under human/operator control.**

## 🔄 Core Workflow

```text
TLE / Tracking Data
        ↓
SGP4 Orbit Propagation
        ↓
Conjunction Screening
        ↓
Miss Distance + Relative Geometry
        ↓
AI Risk Assessment
        ↓
Monte Carlo Uncertainty Analysis
        ↓
Collision Probability
        ↓
Maneuver Optimization
        ↓
Decision & Command Support
```
